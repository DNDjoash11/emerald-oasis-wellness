# FAQ

### How do I book a massage?

Click **Book Now** and choose your preferred service and appointment time.

### How long are the sessions?

Sessions are available in different durations depending on the treatment.

### Can I request a specific area to focus on?

Yes. Let your therapist know which areas you'd like to focus on before your session.

### What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothing. Your therapist will explain everything you need to know before your treatment.

### Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to change your appointment.

### Do I need to arrive early?

Arriving approximately 5–10 minutes before your appointment is recommended.
